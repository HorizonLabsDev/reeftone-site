Frequently asked questions

The result is not saved. Where is my video?

Reef Tone saves a corrected video only when you choose to save it, to Photos or to Files. Your original is never changed. If you left the app before saving, import the video again and repeat the correction.

How do I get natural colors?

Choose the water type that matches what you saw, Blue or Green, then raise the intensity step by step and compare with the original. Advanced mode adds saturation, contrast and dehazing. See the color correction guide.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro screen in Reef Tone and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Reef Tone and cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I bought Lifetime but still see a subscription charge.

Apple treats the subscription and the Lifetime purchase as separate products. If you had a subscription before buying Lifetime, cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Lifetime stays active.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Videos are processed on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Refunds

Refunds for purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
