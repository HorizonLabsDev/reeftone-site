Color correction for DJI Osmo Action underwater footage

Works with any camera

Underwater footage from a DJI Osmo Action

Action cameras like the DJI Osmo Action series, including the Osmo Action 5 Pro, record what reaches the lens. Below a few meters the red and orange are already gone from the light, so the footage looks blue or green. Color correction adds those colors back afterwards.

How to correct DJI footage with Reef Tone

  1. Copy your clips to your iPhone so they appear in Photos, or save them to Files.
  2. Open Reef Tone and import the video from Photos or Files.
  3. Choose the water: Blue or Green, depending on what the water looked like.
  4. Set the intensity and compare with the original before you save.
  5. Save the result to Photos or Files.

Reef Tone works with any video you can import from Photos or Files, so it is not tied to one camera. It has been tested with footage from DJI cameras. Everything is processed on your iPhone, with no account and no upload.

Tips for DJI underwater clips

  • Check the water type per dive. A blue reef and a green lake need different settings.
  • Keep the original clips. Reef Tone saves the corrected video only when you choose to save it, so your originals stay untouched.
  • Get close and use the light. Shallow water and sunshine keep the most color, and video lights help below about 10 m.
  • Process several clips together with batch processing in Reef Tone Pro (up to 10 videos at once).
